👻 Haunted House

On a rainy evening, four friends decided to explore an old abandoned house at the edge of their village. The villagers believed that strange sounds came from the house every night, but no one had the courage to enter it. As the friends pushed open the old wooden door, it creaked loudly. Dust filled the air, and cobwebs covered every corner. Suddenly, they heard footsteps upstairs. With shaking hands, one of them switched on a flashlight and slowly climbed the staircase. The footsteps stopped. Everything became silent. Just as they turned around, a pale white shadow appeared at the end of the hallway. The friends screamed and ran out of the house as fast as they could. The next morning, they returned with the villagers. The house was completely empty. Only one old photograph lay on the floor. No one ever discovered who—or what—they had seen that night. From that day on, the haunted house remained untouched, and everyone in the village avoided it after sunset.
